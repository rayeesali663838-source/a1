About Coolmax River

An independent outdoor guide for runners, hikers, paddlers and travellers who want comfortable feet wherever the trail goes.

Our story

A soggy river crossing

Coolmax River started on a summer hike that included three unexpected river crossings. The walkers in thin synthetic socks were dry and comfortable within half an hour; the ones in thick cotton squelched for the rest of the day.

That afternoon sparked a deep interest in how socks are made and why some work so much better than others. This website shares what we’ve learned from research, testing and a lot of time on trails.

Independent

We do not sell socks and are not affiliated with any fibre maker, sock brand or retailer.

Trail-tested

Our advice draws on real use in heat, rain, rivers and long days on foot.

Practical

We focus on features and habits that make a real difference to comfort.

How we work

Research and real-world use

Our guides combine textile and outdoor references with feedback from runners, hikers and paddlers. We describe how materials and features generally perform, rather than reviewing or ranking products.
