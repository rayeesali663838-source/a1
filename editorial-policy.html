Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Coolmax River guide.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Readers rely on Coolmax River for clear, accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment, free products or gifts in exchange for coverage, and our guides are brand-neutral. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

We draw on textile and outdoor references, care-labelling guidance and the experience of runners, hikers and paddlers. Technical facts are checked against recognised sources.

Responsible writing

  • We describe how fibres and features generally perform rather than making absolute claims.
  • We do not make medical claims about socks.
  • We explain technical terms in plain language.
  • We encourage safe behaviour outdoors, especially around water.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools are created by us and offer general guidance only.
